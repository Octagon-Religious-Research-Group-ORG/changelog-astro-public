Three directions, living together

Renan T, who develops ORG's website, drew up three proposed looks for orgspirituality.org: Night Sanctuary, Prism Light, and Stone & Lapis. That proposal was the spark for ORG's new builds. It's tabled now, and the new builds wear a look of their own, but the three directions never left: they live on inside it as the lights you can choose on every ORG page.

Design directions by Renan T. Kept here as part of ORG's history, with credit to Renan, at Tripp's request. See the original proposal.

How it went

  1. Oct 5, 2026Renan shares three proposed looks for orgspirituality.org, shown on the home and Beliefs pages with ORG's real images and words, and offers them to members for a vote.
  2. Oct 5, 2026The dApp preview in Christopher's lab carries all three at once, with marbled ink you can stir.
  3. Oct 12, 2026Every ORG page on GitHub offers the three as lights, chosen the moment you arrive.
  4. Oct 12, 2026The proposal is tabled. The new builds grow a look of their own, the three directions live on inside it, and this page keeps their story.

Help decide what comes next

The question now isn't which of the three looks wins. It's how these new builds come together with the main site, orgspirituality.org. The likely direction is a new, web3-ready site, rather than adding web3 onto the current one.
