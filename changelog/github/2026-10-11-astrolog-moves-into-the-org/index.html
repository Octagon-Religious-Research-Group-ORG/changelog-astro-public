The AstroLog

ORG now keeps its own record of what changed, owned by the organization rather than a personal account. Entries are short summaries with the full story underneath, grouped into four areas: the live site, the API, ORG on GitHub, and previews. A library holds longer write-ups. You can read it in any of the three proposed lights, and the ink behind the page moves with your pointer (pause it from the header). The library write-up How this AstroLog works covers how to add an entry.

The root page

The root page keeps the animated octagon and adds the nine doors from orgspirituality.org as one perforated sheet, plus links into this AstroLog. Its not-found page now always leads back home, however deep the broken link was.

Shared in Telegram, iMessage or Discord, it arrives with its own preview card.
