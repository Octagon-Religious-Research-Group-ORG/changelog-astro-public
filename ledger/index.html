Ledger

Every public change to ORG's code, in plain words, for anyone who'd rather not open GitHub. Each line is one change, sorted into a kind, credited by first name and last initial, and linked to the change itself. It's gathered from the history of ORG's repositories and refreshed every day.

Work in ORG's private repositories shows too, behind the curtain: the repository, the day and the kind of change, with its words masked (████ ██ ██████) and no link. When an AI helped, its name is on the line below the person's.

The changelog tells the story of what changed and why. The ledger is the step-by-step record underneath it.

131 public changes across 7 repositories, from May 11, 2026 to Oct 22, 2026. Also 15 private changes in 2 private repositories, shown masked: where and when the work happened and what kind it was, never its words. Left out as noise: 6 merges, 1 automatic publish, 3 bot chores, 4 changes that cancelled out (a change and its undo), 8 private changes already shown through a public copy.
