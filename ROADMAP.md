## 🤝 Stewardship and succession

So these pages outlive any one person (today drasticstatic, Christopher W, owns or administers the GitHub
organization and holds the tokens that publish the AstroLog, the lab and the dApp preview). Details
on the [sustainability](https://octagon-religious-research-group-org.github.io/changelog-astro-public/sustainability) page.

- [ ] At least two owners for the GitHub organization, from ORG's own people
- [ ] Publishing through org-owned automation (a GitHub App, or an org bot account), not personal tokens
- [ ] A token register: every token and secret, its owner and its expiry date
- [ ] A renewal runbook, with a reminder a month before each expiry
- [ ] Hand-over steps written down: adding an owner, transferring repositories, domains and the wallet-connect project
- [ ] Exports and backups of the repositories and anything outside GitHub, on a schedule
- [ ] Wallet connect: ORG's own Reown project (a one-line `org.js` change, `give.reownProjectId`), before any treasury address is published
- [ ] Licenses for `org-frontend`, `org-backend` and `ORG-Website`, if ORG wants them

## 🌌 Later, and still being explored

- **A welcome from Tripp.** The welcome guide keeps a space for a few words from ORG's founder,
  empty on purpose until he'd like to write them.
- **A network of our own.** A small social network ORG's members own, on open protocols like Lens
  and Farcaster, paired with a DAO where members propose and vote, the way ORG already decides its
  beliefs. Privacy first. Telegram stays the place to talk in the meantime. A direction, not a
  promise.
