# 🧭 Roadmap

<div class="tldr"><i class="ph ph-path"></i> <strong>TL;DR:</strong> ORG's GitHub home is an entry portal, this AstroLog, Christopher's lab and a dApp preview. What comes next is bringing those new builds together with orgspirituality.org, most likely as a new, web3-ready site. No dates: each step lands in the changelog when it's ready.</div>

Where ORG's GitHub home and its new builds are headed, in rough order. What's already done is in the
[changelog](https://octagon-religious-research-group-org.github.io/changelog-astro-public/changelog),
and what keeps it all running is on the
[sustainability](https://octagon-religious-research-group-org.github.io/changelog-astro-public/sustainability)
page. This is also the repository's `ROADMAP.md`.

## ✅ Just landed

- **A full navbar on every page**, folding into a Menu on smaller screens, with an icon on every link.
- **One window for every way to reach ORG** (its addresses, Tripp on Telegram or Signal, and
  [`CONTACTS.md`](https://github.com/Octagon-Religious-Research-Group-ORG/.github/blob/main/CONTACTS.md)),
  and one for where ORG gathers: its Telegram group.
- **A [map](https://octagon-religious-research-group-org.github.io/changelog-astro-public/map)** of
  every place ORG's pages lead, each with its own picture, above the diagram of how they connect.
- **A [donate page](https://octagon-religious-research-group-org.github.io/donate/)** pointing to ORG's
  own giving page, with a thank-you list for those who wish to be named.

## 🔜 Next

- **The new builds and orgspirituality.org, together.** These pages, the
  [drasticstatic ORG lab](https://drasticstatic.github.io/O-R-G-astro-public/) and its
  [dApp preview](https://drasticstatic.github.io/O-R-G-dapp-public-preview/) began with Renan T's
  design proposal. The open question is how they come together with
  [orgspirituality.org](https://orgspirituality.org). The likely direction is a new, web3-ready site
  rather than adding web3 onto the current one.
  [Help decide what comes next](https://octagon-religious-research-group-org.github.io/changelog-astro-public/three-directions#what-comes-next).
- **Give from a crypto wallet** on the donate page, through the same wallet connection the dApp
  preview uses. The slot is there now, empty on purpose until it's ready.
- **An ORG help desk** for questions and website feedback, with Telegram built in. Until then,
  feedback goes through ORG's Telegram group and the addresses in the Reach out window.

## 🌌 Later, and still being explored

- **A welcome from Tripp.** The welcome guide keeps a space for a few words from ORG's founder,
  empty on purpose until he'd like to write them.
- **A network of our own.** A small social network ORG's members own, on open protocols like Lens
  and Farcaster, paired with a DAO where members propose and vote, the way ORG already decides its
  beliefs. Privacy first. Telegram stays the place to talk in the meantime. A direction, not a
  promise.
